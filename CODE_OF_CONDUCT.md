# Code of Conduct

Rowlith is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions on their merits.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sexualised attention, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related events.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behaviour is abusive or disruptive. To report a concern, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.
